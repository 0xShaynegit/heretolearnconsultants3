We are specialists in

Eight areas of training.

  1. Assessment for learning

    Assessment for learning checks students’ understanding, knowledge and skills during learning rather than only at the end. We have many years of experience helping teachers become real collaborators with their learners, which lifts both engagement and achievement.

  2. Classroom management

    Learning cannot happen without it, and yet so many schools struggle. We help teachers manage classrooms in ways that put children’s emotional wellbeing first while letting teachers keep their cool in challenging moments. A win for everyone. Our training draws on a wide range of techniques grounded in contemporary educational thinking.

  3. Differentiation

    Meeting the needs of every learner in the room is one of the hardest parts of teaching. Our approach encourages teachers to become risk takers and to build a wider repertoire of skills, so learners become more engaged and take more responsibility for their own learning.

  4. Enquiry-based learning

    The hum of a purposeful classroom. Students asking questions, chasing answers and taking charge of their own learning. That is enquiry-based learning in action. It gives learning meaning and raises teaching standards, and our consultants have developed whole-school approaches to it in China and Thailand.

  5. Values-based education

    Schools often tell us they want more balance and a more holistic approach to learning. Values-based Education answers that by changing a school’s philosophy and approach. We have embedded it in schools through whole-school workshops and training.

  6. Play-based learning

    Play-based learning has become popular in early years programmes, but building a play environment that genuinely supports learning is not easy. We help schools move from more traditional teaching to classrooms where children lead their own learning through play and teachers become facilitators.

  7. Learning in the early years

    The first years of school lay the foundations for a happy, successful life. We have set up and led early years programmes and a school, and we offer fresh thinking for this age group, from designing the environment and planning phonics to teaching strategies and assessment.

  8. Mindfulness in the classroom

    The key to a happy classroom is a happy teacher. Mindfulness helps children with focus and managing emotions, and it helps teachers stay calm and composed when things get difficult. We have led mindfulness training for many years, in schools and beyond them.
