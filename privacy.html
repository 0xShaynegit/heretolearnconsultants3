Privacy policy

We are committed to protecting your privacy and keeping your educational institution’s information secure. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect your data when you use our services and visit our website.

Information we collect

  • School information. We may collect information about your school, including its name, location, contact details and specific needs or requirements, when you engage our consultancy services.
  • Contact information. We may collect personal contact information from individuals at your school, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers and job titles.
  • Payment information. If you pay for our services, we may collect payment card information or other payment details.

How we use your information

  • Consultancy services. We use the information you share about your school to deliver our consultancy services, including help with set-up, support with the next stage of development, and professional development.
  • Communication. We may use your contact information to send important updates, reports and information related to our services.

Disclosure of your information

We do not sell, trade or rent your institution’s information, or information about members of your staff, to third parties. We may share your data with trusted partners or experts within our network who help us provide our consultancy services, and they are bound by confidentiality agreements.

Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Please be aware that no method of transmitting data over the internet is entirely secure.
