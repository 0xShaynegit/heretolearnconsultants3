01 / Who we are

Here to Learn is more than our name. We treat every success and every setback as something to learn from, and we are here to help students, teachers, parents and leaders keep learning too.

We are a network of education consultants working with schools across Asia. Between us we have spent many years in classrooms and in the principal’s office, and our work covers school start-up, policy and curriculum, leadership coaching, people management, school improvement and strategic growth.

We believe children learn best when they feel valued, and that kindness, empathy and encouragement are not soft extras. They are the conditions that make real achievement possible.

Getting you started

  • Vision and missionPlanning and visioning days that get your whole team pulling in the same direction.
  • Organisational planningStructures, roles and lines of communication designed before day one, not patched afterwards.
  • Curriculum designExperience with all the major international curricula, from adapting one to writing your own.
  • RecruitmentHiring for character and values as carefully as for qualifications.
  • School registrationStep by step support through the process of registering a school in Thailand.
  • Financial forecastingShort and long-term forecasts, built from the real costs of running a school.

Where we specialise

Professional development in full →
  • Assessment for learningChecking understanding during learning, not only at the end, so teachers and students work it out together.
  • Classroom managementApproaches that protect children’s wellbeing and help teachers keep their cool.
  • DifferentiationHelping teachers become confident risk takers who can reach every learner in the room.
  • Enquiry-based learningWhole-school approaches to enquiry, developed in schools in China and Thailand.
  • Values-based educationA whole-school shift towards balance and a more holistic approach to learning.
  • Play-based learningMoving from traditional teaching to classrooms where children lead their own learning through play.
  • Learning in the early yearsEnvironment, phonics, teaching and assessment for the years that shape everything after.
  • Mindfulness in the classroomA happy classroom starts with a happy teacher. We have taught mindfulness for many years.
