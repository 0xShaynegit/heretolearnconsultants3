Our team’s impact

Work our consultants have led.

  1. Start-up

    Led two new international schools from opening, grew enrolment significantly in their first three years and established both in the regional international school market.

  2. Start-up

    Founded a play-based preschool that reached full enrolment in its very first term.

    Chiang Mai Grow-operative, Thailand
  3. Start-up

    Founded an English language school in northern Thailand that is still running successfully today.

    The Learning Centre, Thailand
  4. Improvement

    Established and implemented the Cambridge Primary Curriculum for a new international school.

    Meritton British International School, Thailand
  5. Improvement

    Introduced and embedded enquiry-based learning as part of whole-school development, which brought more meaning, and more fun, to learning.

    Meritton British International School, Thailand
  6. Improvement

    Carried out a whole-school assessment and strategy to embed values-based learning across the school.

    Meritton British International School, Thailand
  7. Improvement

    Wrote medium and long-term school improvement plans for two international schools.

    Chiang Mai, Thailand
  8. Improvement

    Wrote medium and long-term budget forecasts for two new international school start-ups.

    Thailand
  9. Improvement

    Reviewed and renewed a code of conduct, leading to an effective policy on interventions and community conduct built on respect, kindness and compassion.

  10. Training

    Coached and mentored principals, senior and middle leaders and teachers in schools in Thailand, China and New Zealand.

  11. Training

    Led teacher training for different schools on pedagogy, classroom management, school marketing, mindfulness, values-based education and more.

  12. Training

    Ran a series of whole-school workshops to raise standards of English among teaching staff, and saw their confidence in using English grow.

    Tessaban 1, northern Thailand
