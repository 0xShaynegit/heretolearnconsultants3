What it takes to set up a new school

Gary Jolliffe  /  8 December 2023  /  2 min read

Setting up a new school is a big undertaking. It takes careful planning, thoughtful decisions, expert advice and a great deal of commitment. These are the key questions you will need to work through along the way.

Philosophy and vision

  • What are your core values and beliefs about education? Once you have defined them, they will help you establish your school’s mission.
  • What vision captures your school’s own approach to teaching and learning?
  • Remember that your vision is a public statement. It tells your community what the school is for and where it is heading.

Pedagogy

  • What is the best approach to teaching? How do children learn best?
  • Make your chosen pedagogy a central part of your mission. It is how you will achieve your vision.

Curriculum design

  • Which curriculum best fits your educational philosophy? Remember that it needs to be balanced and holistic.
  • Will it meet, or exceed, the standards you expect?

Assessment and evaluation

  • Develop effective ways to assess students and track their progress.
  • Review teaching practice and the curriculum regularly to keep standards high.

Legal and regulatory compliance

  • Does your school comply with national regulations for starting and running an educational facility?
  • Which licences, permits and accreditations do you need to be legitimate and credible?

Facilities and infrastructure

  • Will you be one-form or two-form entry? Libraries, canteens and other shared spaces are quickly outgrown.
  • What facilities and design will create a learning environment that reflects your approach?

Staffing

  • You will be competing with established schools for staff. What will make yours attractive?
  • How will you make sure the teachers you hire share the school’s vision and values?
  • Which support staff do you need for the school to run smoothly?

Financial planning and budgeting

  • What belongs in a detailed budget that covers set-up costs, ongoing running costs and contingencies?

Admissions and enrolment

  • New parents will judge you by how easy and efficient your admissions process is. How can you make it as smooth and professional as possible?
  • What will your fees include? Tuition, lunches, learning resources, trips?
  • Will you offer scholarships, and if so, what kind?

Technology

  • How will technology be part of the curriculum?
  • What do you need for an IT infrastructure that is both appropriate and cyber-safe?

Marketing and communication

  • What brand identity and marketing strategy will attract students and stakeholders?
  • What will keep communication clear, so that parents, students and staff stay well informed?

Continuous improvement

  • How will you build a culture of continuous improvement?

Setting up a new school takes commitment, passion and dedication. It also takes the judgement to know when to ask for expert help. A school is built by a team, with each person bringing their own skills and experience to the table.
