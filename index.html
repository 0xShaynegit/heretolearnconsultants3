01 / Who we are

Here to Learn is more than our name. We treat every success and every setback as something to learn from, and we are here to help students, teachers, parents and leaders keep learning too.

We are a network of education consultants working with schools across Asia. Between us we have spent many years in classrooms and in the principal’s office, and our work covers school start-up, policy and curriculum, leadership coaching, people management, school improvement and strategic growth.

We believe children learn best when they feel valued, and that kindness, empathy and encouragement are not soft extras. They are the conditions that make real achievement possible.

Where we specialise

Professional development in full →
  • Assessment for learningChecking understanding during learning, not only at the end, so teachers and students work it out together.
  • Classroom managementApproaches that protect children’s wellbeing and help teachers keep their cool.
  • DifferentiationHelping teachers become confident risk takers who can reach every learner in the room.
