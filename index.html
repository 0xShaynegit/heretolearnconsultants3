TECHNICAL FAQ

Straight answers, no fine print theatre.

Q-01What does a mortgage broker cost me?+

Nothing. Brokers are paid a commission by the lender on settlement. Under the Best Interests Duty we are legally required to act in your interests, not the lender's, and our recommendation document discloses every commission.

Q-02How much deposit do I actually need?+

As little as 5% with the First Home Guarantee, or 2% for eligible single parents. Outside the schemes, anything under 20% typically attracts Lenders Mortgage Insurance, which we model into the comparison so you can see the true cost.

Q-03Will shopping around damage my credit score?+

Multiple direct applications will. Our comparison work happens off-file: we assess policy fit across the panel first, then lodge one application with the right lender. One enquiry, not six.

Q-04Fixed or variable in the current market?+

Usually neither on its own. Split facilities let you fix a portion for certainty and keep a variable portion with an offset. The right ratio depends on your cash buffer and how long you will hold the property. We model both.

Q-05How long does approval take?+

Pre-approval in 2 to 10 business days depending on lender queue. Full approval to settlement typically runs 21 to 35 days. We publish current lender turnaround times to every client weekly.
