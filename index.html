HOW WE WORK

Four stages, built around your school.

  1. STAGE 01

    Listen

    We start with conversations: leaders, staff and, where it helps, students and parents. What is working, what is not, and what you want to be true next year.

    CONVERSATIONS
  2. STAGE 02

    Understand

    We look at the evidence with the people closest to it, in classrooms and in documents, and agree together what matters most.

    EVIDENCE
  3. STAGE 03

    Plan

    A clear written plan with owners, timelines and a shared picture of what success looks like. Short enough that people actually use it.

    WRITTEN PLAN
  4. STAGE 04

    Support

    Coaching, training and regular check-ins while the plan becomes everyday practice. Online, on site, or a mix of both.

    ONLINE OR ON SITE

Between us we have spent many years in the classroom and in the principal’s office. We know what a Monday morning feels like.

QUESTIONS

What schools usually ask first.

Q-01Do you work online or in person?+

Both. Coaching and advice often work well online. Reviews, training days and start-up planning usually benefit from time on site. Many schools use a mix.

Q-02Where do you work?+

We support schools across the Asia region. Tell us where you are and we will tell you how we can work with you there.

Q-03Can you help before our school has opened?+

Yes. School start-up is one of our core areas: vision, structures, policies, curriculum and staffing, planned before the first student arrives.

Q-04Who will we actually work with?+

Consultants from our network with classroom and school leadership experience in the area you need. We introduce them before any work begins.

Q-05How much does it cost?+

It depends on what you need, from a single advice session to a full project. We quote once we understand your school, so the first step is a conversation.
